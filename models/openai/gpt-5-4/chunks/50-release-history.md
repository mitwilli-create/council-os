**Summary** — GPT-5.4 was released March 5, 2026, succeeding GPT-5.2. GPT-5.5 — a fully retrained agentic model with clear benchmark gains — followed approximately seven weeks later on April 23, 2026. The rapid successor cadence signals GPT-5.4 is a stable-but-non-flagship routing option rather than the long-term top tier.

**Specifics:**
- **GPT-5.4 release date:** March 5, 2026. ([pricepertoken.com](https://pricepertoken.com/pricing-page/model/openai-gpt-5.4))
- **Predecessor:** GPT-5.2. OpenAI prompt guidance frames GPT-5.4 as "new in GPT-5.4 vs GPT-5.2" with migration guidance from `gpt-5.2` to `gpt-5.4`. ([OpenAI prompt guidance](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/prompt-guidance))
- **Successor:** GPT-5.5, released April 23, 2026 — approximately 7 weeks after GPT-5.4. ([marktechpost.com](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/04/23/openai-releases-gpt-5-5-a-fully-retrained-agentic-model-that-scores-82-7-on-terminal-bench-2-0-and-84-9-on-gdpval/))
- **GPT-5.2 retirement date:** **[UNKNOWN — would need deprecation notice]**.

**Compared to peers (sharpened by Dealbreaker):**
- vs. openai/gpt-5-5: GPT-5.4 is the prior generation; GPT-5.5 outperforms on 9/10 benchmarks and was released only 7 weeks later.
